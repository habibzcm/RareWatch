RareWatch - third-party data notice
===================================

RareData.lua is generated from:

1. QuestieDB - https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
   File: data/Forever/foreverNpcDB.lua (NPC names, levels, rank, Forever map coordinates)
   and data/Forever/conversion.json (zone names and UI map IDs).
   Questie / QuestieDB content is shared by the Questie team under GPLv3 terms
   (see their LICENSE.md for details). "Questie" is their name and logo; RareWatch is
   not affiliated with or endorsed by the Questie team.

2. cmangos classic-db - https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db
   Respawn time estimates (creature.spawntimesecsmin / spawntimesecsmax), GPL.
   These are emulator values, not official Blizzard data, and may differ in WoW Forever.

3. ForeverDB.net - https://foreverdb.net
   Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net). Drop rates "seen in Forever" (measured over
   ForeverDB players' kills in WoW Forever) come from the ForeverDB addon's Data/Rares.lua,
   GPL-3.0 with an attribution term. Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net. RareWatch is not affiliated
   with or endorsed by ForeverDB.net.

DropData.lua is generated from the cmangos classic-db project (creature_loot_template,
reference_loot_template, item_template, creature_template) and ForeverDB.net's rare loot data.
Rows marked "seen" are Forever measurements from ForeverDB; rows marked "new" are items added in Forever whose rates were noted by hand from Wowhead's WoW Forever pages (small samples; Wowhead is not affiliated with RareWatch); all other drop chances are vanilla emulator values
and may differ in WoW Forever.
